## Summary

I designed **STRUM (Strum Tracking via Reflected Ultrasound on Mobiles)** as a project for the Fall 2026 **Mobile Computing and Its Applications** course at Seoul National University. The proposed interface combines touchscreen chord selection with bare-hand, mid-air strumming using a single smartphone.

**Status:** Project proposal and system design; implementation is planned. The repository currently contains the proposal and illustration assets.

![Overall workflow of STRUM: mid-air strumming, acoustic hand tracking, gesture recognition, and guitar sound output.]({{ '/assets/strum/strum-teaser.png' | relative_url }})

## Overview

- **Target device:** Galaxy S24+ (Android)
- **Interaction:** Select chords with one hand and strum in mid-air with the other.
- **Goal:** Provide a portable, two-handed guitar-playing interface without additional equipment.

## Proposed Architecture

The planned pipeline has four stages:

1. **Data collection:** Transmit multi-frequency acoustic signals and record reflections from the hand.
2. **Hand tracking:** Use LLAP-based signal processing to estimate 2D hand trajectories.
3. **ML-based inference:** Detect strums and estimate direction and relative intensity from sliding-window trajectories.
4. **Guitar sound output:** Combine the recognized strumming information with the chord selected on the touchscreen.

![STRUM system architecture, including acoustic sensing, hand tracking, ML inference, and the guitar audio engine.]({{ '/assets/strum/system-architecture.png' | relative_url }})

## Implementation and Evaluation Plan

The proposal targets an Android prototype with Kotlin and C++ signal processing, self-collected motion data, and lightweight on-device models. Key challenges include phone motion, reflections from the body and surroundings, and responsive sound output.

The planned evaluation compares strum/non-strum detection accuracy and model inference latency before and after optimization, including quantization and pruning. These are planned experiments, with results to be added after implementation.
